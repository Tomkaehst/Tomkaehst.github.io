Postdoctoral researcher in single-molecule biophysics &middot; Jena, Germany

I study how biomolecules move using single-molecule FRET (smFRET) and fluorescence spectroscopy. Watching individual molecules under near-native conditions lets us disentangle conformational states that ensemble methods average away.

I am a postdoc in the [Dienerowitz group](https://www.eah-jena.de/scitec/personen/dienerowitz-maria) at Ernst-Abbe-Hochschule Jena and did my PhD in the [Dynamic Bioimaging Lab](https://www.uhasselt.be/en/onderzoeksgroepen-en/dynamic-bioimaging-lab) at Hasselt University. See my [CV](/cv/) for details.

## Background

- **Postdoc**, [Dienerowitz group](https://www.eah-jena.de/scitec/personen/dienerowitz-maria), Ernst-Abbe-Hochschule Jena (2026–present) — smFRET instrumentation and biomolecular dynamics within the DFG-funded [TOOLS project](https://tools-jena.de) (Tailored Optics for Life Sciences Engineering)
- **PhD** (defended September 2026), [Dynamic Bioimaging Lab](https://www.uhasselt.be/en/onderzoeksgroepen-en/dynamic-bioimaging-lab) of Prof. Dr. Jelle Hendrix, Hasselt University (Belgium) — DNA ligase dynamics via smFRET, FWO doctoral fellow
- **MSc**, Biochemistry, Friedrich Schiller University Jena
- **BSc**, Biology, Friedrich Schiller University Jena

## Research interests

- Single-molecule fluorescence spectroscopy: smFRET, FCS, and single-photon counting methods
- Conformational dynamics of biomolecules
- Quantitative analysis of photon-by-photon smFRET data
